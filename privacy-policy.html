Forest Drift

Privacy Policy · Last updated: 13 October 2026

In short
  • Forest Drift works fully offline. It has no account, no ads and no analytics.
  • The game does not collect, sell or share your personal information.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device.

Who we are

Forest Drift is a game made by Nadesoft ("we", "us"). You can reach us at contact@nadesoft.bd (replace with your support address before publishing).

What stays on your device

  • Save data: your progress, unlocked items, settings and goals are stored in the game's private storage on your device. We cannot see them. Uninstalling the game deletes them.
  • Photos: in photo mode, a picture is saved to your gallery only when you tap Save, and shared only through your device's own share sheet when you choose to. The game does not upload photos. Android 8 and 9 may ask for storage permission the first time you save.
  • Road codes and ghost links: when you share a road or a ghost, the code or link is passed to the app you pick in the share sheet. The game itself sends nothing.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No location, contacts, microphone or camera access.
  • No sale of data, because we do not have any.

Optional online features

Some builds may offer leaderboards or community goals through your platform's own game service (Google Play Games on Android, Game Center on iOS). These are optional. If you choose to use them, the platform handles sign-in and the data under its own privacy policy: Google and Apple. Forest Drift works the same without them.

Children

The game has no chat and no content shared with other players. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. If a future version adds data collection, we will say so here and in the store listing before it ships.

Contact
